# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 29, 2025

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sign-Speak-AI, our South African Sign Language (SASL) detection web application ("the Application," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with this university research project.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. By using this Application, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Camera Data
- **Real-time Video Input:** The Application accesses your device camera to capture real-time video for sign language detection
- **Processing:** Video frames are processed locally in your browser to detect hand gestures and SASL signs
- **Storage:** Video data is NOT stored permanently. Video frames are processed in real-time and discarded immediately after processing

### 2.2 Technical Information
We may automatically collect:
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
- Screen resolution
- Time and date of access
- Pages or features accessed within the Application
- Performance metrics (processing time, detection accuracy rates)

### 2.3 Usage Data
- Sign detection results (text output)
- Interaction patterns with the Application
- Error logs and diagnostic information
- Session duration and frequency of use

### 2.4 Voluntary Information
- Feedback or comments you choose to provide
- Survey responses if you participate in research studies
- Demographic information (only if voluntarily provided for research purposes)

## 3. How We Use Information

### 3.1 Primary Purposes
- **Functionality:** To provide sign language detection and text-to-speech conversion services
- **Improvement:** To analyze and improve detection accuracy and application performance
- **Research:** To conduct academic research on sign language recognition technology
- **Development:** To identify bugs, errors, and areas for technical enhancement

### 3.2 Academic Research
As a university project, collected data may be used for:
- Academic publications and presentations
- Development of improved sign language detection algorithms
- Statistical analysis of application usage patterns
- Educational purposes within the university
- Thesis or dissertation research

All research use will involve anonymized and aggregated data unless explicit consent is obtained for other uses.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Local Processing
- Video processing occurs locally in your browser
- Camera feeds are NOT transmitted to external servers during normal operation
- No video recordings are saved unless you explicitly grant permission

### 4.2 Data Retention
- **Session Data:** Cleared when you close the browser or end your session
- **Usage Statistics:** May be retained in anonymized form for research purposes
- **Technical Logs:** Retained only as long as necessary for debugging and improvement purposes

### 4.3 Security Measures
We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information:
- Secure browser connections (HTTPS where available)
- No permanent storage of sensitive camera data
- Limited data collection practices
- Access controls for any stored data

However, as this is an educational project, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Use the Application at your own risk.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 No Commercial Sharing
We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for commercial purposes.

### 5.2 Academic Sharing

### 5.3 Legal Requirements

### 5.4 Third-Party Services
The Application may use third-party libraries or services (e.g., machine learning frameworks, text-to-speech APIs). These services may have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Camera Access Control
- You can deny or revoke camera access at any time through your browser settings
- Denying camera access will prevent the Application from functioning

### 6.2 Browser Controls
- Clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings
- Use private/incognito browsing mode
- Disable JavaScript (though this will prevent the Application from working)

### 6.3 Data Deletion
- Since most data is processed locally and not stored permanently, closing your browser session deletes most information
- For any stored usage statistics, you may request deletion by contacting us

### 6.4 Opt-Out
You may opt out of using the Application at any time by simply closing it and not returning.

## 7. Cookies and Local Storage

The Application may use:

- **Local Storage:** May store user preferences or settings
- **Cookies:** May be used for session management or analytics 

You can control cookies and storage through your browser settings.
